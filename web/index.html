THE FOUR

CINDERWARD Ember Knight
Straight sword, steady pressure. The baseline — every number average, nothing to work around. Learn the spacing here.
PALE VIGIL Spectral Sentinel
Longest reach and a faster walk. Wins the poking game and loses the trade if you let anyone inside it.
BRONZEMAW Verdigris Brute
Heaviest damage, slowest recovery. A whiffed heavy is a free punish for the other side, so make them come to you.
NOCTURNE Violet Cutthroat
Quickest on the stage and the most fragile. Built for dash-ins and for leaving before the answer arrives.

THE MOVES

LIGHT 7 dmg · fast
Short startup, short recovery. Your check, and the link that starts most combos.
HEAVY 12 dmg · launches
Slow enough to be seen coming. Big knockback, so it ends a combo rather than continuing one.
ASSIST 50% meter
Both deal 10. Emberwraith splits it: 6 on the leap, 4 on a cinder orb that flies on. The Carrion Idol lands all 10 in one slam whose shockwave travels the floor, later but harder to escape. Six-second lockout.
OVERDRIVE 100% meter
Eight seconds at double speed, trailing light. Spends the whole bar, so it is Overdrive or two assists, never both.

COMBO TIPS

  • Light into heavy is the bread and butter. Light recovers fast enough that heavy connects before they can guard again.
  • Jump-ins carry your momentum. Both attacks stay live in the air and keep the speed you jumped with — dash, then jump, then hit, and the whole thing lands as one.
  • Blocking breaks the attacker's combo. Guarding cuts most of the damage and kills the knockback, but only while you are facing the blow. Turn your back and it is a clean hit.
  • Dash is a double-tap, and it has recovery. Dashing past someone turns you around; dashing into a heavy is how rounds end.
  • Meter builds on hits given and taken. Losing a round is not the worst thing that can happen to your bar.
